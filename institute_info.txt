FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Is online class available?
A: Yes, for Class 11 to 12 evening batch only. Recorded lectures are shared the same day.

Q: What if my child misses a class?
A: Notes and a short recap are available at the office, and the teacher can clear doubts on Saturday.

Q: Can we change batch after joining?
A: Yes, once, within the first month, subject to seat availability.

Q: Is there a refund if we leave midway?
A: Registration fee is non-refundable. For remaining fees, a refund of unused full months is given if you inform us in writing at least 15 days before leaving.

Q: Do you teach CBSE or State Board?
A: Both CBSE and State Board syllabus are covered.

Q: Do you offer a scholarship?
A: A 20% fee concession is offered to students scoring above 90% in our entrance test. Seats are limited.

Q: Can my child attend only one subject?
A: Yes, single-subject enrollment is available. Please share the class and subject for the exact fee.

Q: Is hostel or transport available?
A: No hostel. Transport is not provided, but the centre is near the main bus stop.
